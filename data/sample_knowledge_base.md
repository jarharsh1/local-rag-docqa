> it with your own corpus — policies, manuals, contracts, protocols, FAQs — and

## Refunds
A customer may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are issued
to the original payment method within five business days of approval. Digital
goods are non-refundable once downloaded.

## Service-level agreement
Standard support tickets receive a first response within 24 hours. Priority
incidents affecting production are acknowledged within one hour and are updated
every four hours until resolved.

## Data retention
Personal data is retained for 24 months after the last account activity, after
which it is anonymized. Customers may request deletion of their data at any time,
which is completed within 30 days.

## Onboarding
New users must verify their email address before accessing the dashboard.
Administrators can invite team members, who inherit the role assigned at the time
of the invitation.

## Security
Passwords must be at least twelve characters and are stored only as salted
hashes. Two-factor authentication is required for all administrator accounts and
is strongly recommended for everyone else.

## Billing
Invoices are issued on the first of each month and are payable within fourteen
days. Accounts more than thirty days overdue are suspended until the balance is
cleared.
